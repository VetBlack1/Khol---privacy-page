# Khol: privacy policy and terms

The public privacy policy and terms of use of Khol, an offline training log and shot timer for dry fire and live fire, in English, Ukrainian, Polish, German, Lithuanian, Latvian and Estonian. Served with GitHub Pages.

The pages are generated from the app's own texts (`dart run tool/build_site.dart` in the app's repository), so the site and the app say the same thing. Do not edit them here by hand.

- Privacy policy: `privacy/` (English), `privacy/<lang>.html`
- Terms of use: `terms/`, `terms/<lang>.html`
