Terms of use

What Khol is

Khol is a training log and a shot timer for dry-fire and live-fire practice. It is not instruction from a qualified instructor, and it is not a safety device: it cannot see your gun, your ammunition or your range.

Who it is for

Khol is for adults (18 and over) who may lawfully own or use a firearm where they live, or who train under a qualified instructor.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for handling firearms safely and lawfully. Follow the firearm laws where you are and the rules of your range. Dry fire only with an unloaded gun and no live ammunition in the room. Wear eye and ear protection for live fire. If a drill is not allowed where you train, or you are not sure you can do it safely, do not do it.

The drills

Drills and standards are general information. They may not suit your firearm, your skill or your range. Some, such as shooting from cover or after moving, are not permitted on every range; in Germany such exercises are not permitted for civilians.
