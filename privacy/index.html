Privacy policy

In short

Khol collects nothing. It has no account, makes no network requests and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your sessions, drills, results, notes and ratings, plans and reminders, gear and ammunition counts, settings and target photos. They live in the app's own storage and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Microphone

When you turn on listening or calibrate Khol Timer, the microphone is analysed on the phone while you train. No audio is recorded, stored or sent. A calibration keeps only a few numbers that describe the sound of your shots.

Camera, photos, calendar and notifications

A target photo is kept with its result on your phone. Adding a session to your calendar opens your calendar app with the event filled in; Khol never reads your calendar. Reminders are notifications scheduled on the phone.

Your exports

A backup or a spreadsheet you export is a file you choose where to send. It is not encrypted; keep it like any personal file.

Mail and Discord

Contact opens your mail app with a message you write and send yourself. The Discord community opens in Discord or your browser. Khol sends nothing itself; what you write there is handled by your mail provider or by Discord under their own privacy policies.

Children

Khol is for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is in the app and at the address in the store listing.
